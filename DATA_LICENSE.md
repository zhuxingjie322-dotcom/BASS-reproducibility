# Data attribution and separate terms

MIT covers author-provided code and documentation. Third-party UCI benchmark files retain CC BY 4.0 terms; MIT does not replace those terms.

- Heart Disease (processed Cleveland): Janosi, Steinbrunn, Pfisterer and Detrano, 1989. UCI. https://doi.org/10.24432/C52P4X ; https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/45/heart+disease . File: corrected_core_20261107/data/processed.cleveland.data. Missing-data rows are removed in code and positive diagnosis is collapsed to 1.
- Breast Cancer Wisconsin (Diagnostic): Wolberg, Mangasarian and Street, 1993. UCI. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5DW2B ; https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/17/breast+cancer+wisconsin+diagnostic . File: corrected_core_20261107/data/wdbc.data. The identifier column is excluded from model inputs.

Data license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

SimSUM-style and DDXPlus-style cohorts are local study-specific approximations, not copies of the corresponding published datasets. No protected clinical cohort or MIMIC records are included. The only raw medical benchmark files are the two public UCI files identified above.
